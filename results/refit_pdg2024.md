# Refit of potential models to PDG 2024

Joint least-squares fit of each potential family (parameters, m_c, m_b, α_s) to 27 measured charmonium and bottomonium levels (J-resolved, below open-flavour threshold), σ² = σ_exp² + (10 MeV)².

Parameter errors are from the covariance of the least-squares fit at the 10 MeV theory floor. For the Martin family B, α, m_c and m_b are strongly correlated (B·r^α ≈ B(1 + α ln r) for small α, so B carries a constant that is degenerate with the quark masses); the individual errors are large but the predicted *masses* are not. The combination m_c + m_b that enters B_c is well constrained by the cc̄ and bb̄ fits.
